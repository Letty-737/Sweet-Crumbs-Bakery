Our Story

A Passion for Baking

Sweet Crumbs Bakery began with a passion for baking and a dream to share joy through delicious treats.

From humble beginnings in Johannesburg, we have grown into a bakery focused on quality, care and creativity.

Every loaf, pastry, cake and cookie is prepared with attention to detail so that our customers can enjoy something truly special.

Meet the Founder

Leticia Hlabane

Sweet Crumbs started in a small home kitchen in Johannesburg, where Leticia spent weekends baking for family birthdays, church gatherings and friends who kept asking for "just one more batch".

What began as a love for her grandmother's recipes soon turned into orders from neighbours and local events. With every cake she decorated, her dream grew: a bakery where every treat is made with care and shared with joy.

Today Leticia leads Sweet Crumbs with the same passion she started with — bringing fresh, beautiful bakes to our community, one celebration at a time.
